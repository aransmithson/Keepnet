**Date:** October 2026  
**Structure:** Strictly Two Tiers — Lite (Free) & Premium (Subscription)  
**Status:** Monetisation Specification & Architectural Blueprint  

---

## 1. Executive Summary

Keepnet uses a simple, transparent two-tier model designed specifically for UK coarse, carp, and game anglers:

1. **Lite (100% Free Forever)**: The essential bankside journal and open community. Anglers can log unlimited catches and sessions, explore all UK fisheries on the Discover map, browse public catch reports, and exchange likes.
2. **Premium (Paid Subscription)**: The Bankside Intelligence and Specimen Suite. Designed for anglers who want deeper tactical advantages—barometric bite prediction, solunar feeding windows, bait & rig performance analytics, syndicate stealth privacy, uncompressed 4K photo storage, and printable PDF catch dossiers.

## 3. Detailed Tier Breakdown

### Tier 1: Lite (100% Free Forever)
*Goal: Remove all barriers to entry, drive rapid word-of-mouth adoption, and foster an active UK angling community.*

- **Unrestricted Bankside Logging**:
  - Log unlimited fishing sessions and catches.
  - Record species, weights (lb/oz or kg/g), baits, swim notes, and real-time weather.
  - View personal bests (PBs) per species and overall bankside hours.
- **Open Discover Directory & Community Map**:
  - Full access to all 60+ researched UK commercial and pleasure day-ticket fisheries.
  - Search by region, town, access type, and target species.
  - View community catch reports and shared angler sessions.
- **Social Tools & Community Likes**:
  - 1-click liking on catch reports.
  - Share prize catches to Discover to inspire fellow anglers.
  - Unlock core milestones and equip avatar flairs (e.g. *Friendly Angler*, *First Catch*, *Specimen Hunter*).
- **Rock-Solid Offline Reliability**:
  - Full functionality with zero mobile reception bankside.
  - Automatic background sync when cell service or Wi-Fi reconnects.
- **Efficient Photo Storage**:
  - Fast-loading, bandwidth-friendly compressed catch photography.

---

### Tier 2: Premium (The Bankside Intelligence & Specimen Suite)
*Goal: Provide tangible, data-driven angling advantages and prestige that serious carp, match, and specimen anglers happily subscribe to.*

#### 1. Atmospheric & Barometric Bite Intelligence
- **Pressure Trend Analysis**:
  - Correlates your catch history with barometric pressure swings (rising, falling, or steady).
  - Identifies which atmospheric conditions trigger aggressive feeding runs for specific species (e.g., carp moving into shallow water during dropping pressure).
- **Solunar Feeding Forecast**:
  - Calculates major and minor daily feeding windows based on solar and lunar positions, moon phases, and localized sunrises/sunsets.
  - Gives anglers a practical "Best Bite Window" for planning session arrival and baiting times.

#### 2. Tactical Bait, Rig & Swim Efficiency Matrix
- **Bait Performance Scorecard**:
  - See which baits produce the highest catch frequency and biggest average weights across all waters (e.g., boilies vs. wafters vs. maggots vs. corn).
- **Rig & Depth Analytics**:
  - Track which hook arrangements, rig mechanics (Ronnie, Chod, Method Feeder, German Rig), and depths yield positive hook-ups.
- **Historical Water Insights**:
  - Review how a specific venue performed across different months, temperatures, and seasons to prepare tackle in advance.

#### 3. Syndicate Stealth Cloak (Secret Water Privacy)
- Built for anglers fishing private syndicates, secret gravel pits, or sensitive river beats where secrecy is paramount:
  - **GPS Air-Gap**: Strips all EXIF GPS tags and venue coordinates automatically.
  - **Cloaked Swims**: Keep detailed private notes and peg locations for personal records without any risk of accidental public leak.

#### 4. Uncompressed 4K Photo Vault
- Unlimited storage of pristine, high-resolution original trophy fish photos.
- Preserves full camera detail for framing, printing, or social media portfolio display.

#### 5. Printable PDF Dossiers & PB Certificates
- **Syndicate Catch Return Dossiers**: Formatted PDF export summarizing season catches, dates, and weights for club bailiffs and syndicate books.
- **Personal Best Showcase Certificates**: High-resolution certificate exports celebrating milestone catches.

#### 6. Premium Prestige & Golden Avatar Ring
- Distinctive Golden Pro badge ring on profile avatar and public catch reports.
- Unlocks the highest Specimen Trophy flairs and badges.

## 4. Approved Pricing & Commercial Model

Unlike the scrapped 99p micro-tier (which lost 23% in processing fees), Premium is priced at an accessible volume-focused price point while preserving healthy unit economics:

| Option | Price (Gross) | Payment Fees (Stripe UK) | Net Take-Home | Effective Monthly Cost |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| **Monthly Subscription** | **£1.99 / month** | ~£0.23 (1.5% + 20p + 0.7%) | **£1.76 / month** (88.4% margin) | £1.99 / month |
| **Annual Subscription** | **£14.99 / year** | ~£0.43 (1.5% + 20p + 0.7%) | **£14.56 / year** (97.1% margin) | **£1.25 / month** (37% discount) |

### Why this pricing works:
1. **Ultra-Low Barrier to Entry**: At £1.99/mo (or ~£1.25/mo on annual), it is cheaper than a single bag of terminal tackle or a tub of sweetcorn, making it a frictionless impulse upgrade for everyday anglers.
2. **Superior Margins to 99p**: Avoids the sub-£1 fee trap; generates **2.3x more net profit per subscriber** than the old 99p model while delivering real tactical value.
3. **High Annual Upfront Cashflow**: £14.99 billed upfront locks in a full year of service with 97.1% net margin and near-zero ongoing renewal friction.

> **Status Note**: This pricing structure is locked as the official Keepnet design specification. Implementation is currently on hold per product owner direction to prioritize core app features and angler experience.

## 5. Technical Implementation Architecture

### 1. Entitlement State (Cloudflare D1)
`users` table in D1 stores the authoritative entitlement status:
```sql
ALTER TABLE users ADD COLUMN subscription_tier TEXT DEFAULT 'lite'; -- 'lite' | 'premium'
ALTER TABLE users ADD COLUMN subscription_status TEXT DEFAULT 'active'; -- 'active' | 'past_due' | 'canceled'
ALTER TABLE users ADD COLUMN subscription_expires_at TEXT;
ALTER TABLE users ADD COLUMN stripe_customer_id TEXT;
ALTER TABLE users ADD COLUMN stripe_subscription_id TEXT;
```

### 2. Client Store Integration (`src/store.ts`)
- The client store tracks:
  - `isPremium: boolean` (derived from validated cloud account status).
- **Lite users** enjoy full unblocked journal logging, Discover map exploration, and catch report likes.
- **Premium users** have the Bankside Intelligence tab, Solunar Predictor, Stealth Cloak, and PDF Dossier exports enabled.
- Lite users clicking on Bankside Intelligence see an interactive preview/teaser showcasing what Premium unlocks, with a 1-click upgrade button.

### 3. Payment Processing Flow (Stripe Hosted Checkout)
- Cloudflare Pages Function `/functions/api/stripe/checkout.ts`:
  - Creates a Stripe Checkout session for authenticated users.
- Cloudflare Pages Function `/functions/api/stripe/webhook.ts`:
  - Authoritatively updates `subscription_tier = 'premium'` upon `checkout.session.completed` or `invoice.paid`.
  - Reverts gracefully to `lite` if subscription lapses or is canceled, **never deleting or locking any of the angler's journal data**.
