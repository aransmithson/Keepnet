KEEPNET MONETISATION PLAN
4 October 2026 | Review only | No implementation authorised

1. OWNER DIRECTION
Keepnet should have free Lite and low-cost Premium. Discover belongs in Premium. The owner rejected £1.99/month and responded positively to 99p/month or £9.99/year. These lower prices are the preferred review direction; final allowances, economics and launch still need approval. Everything below is a proposal except the owner's Premium Discover direction. Inspect current repository rather than assuming earlier architecture remains.

2. RECOMMENDED TIERS
Lite: catch/session journal, notes, species, weights, manual venue/swim names, basic totals and personal bests, modest photo allowance, private personal records, basic export, access to existing history and dependable core saving once verified.
Premium: Discover directory, fishery search/filtering, Discover map and fishery detail pages; potentially larger photo allowance, deeper comparisons by swim/bait/season and polished reports later.
Free users can see a static benefits preview, not the live Discover directory/map. Manual journal venue text remains available. Preserve previously recorded venue names after downgrade. Do not gate reading existing journal records behind payment.
For costing only assess 50MB optimised photos Lite and 500MB Premium. These are unapproved trial assumptions, not promised allowances. Show byte usage. Avoid unlimited cloud storage/lifetime storage deals. Basic data export stays free; polished reports may be paid.
Don't sell planned features before they work.

3. PRICE AND BENCHMARKS
Preferred review prices: £0.99 monthly or £9.99 annually. Annual equivalent £0.8325/month; 15.9% saving against twelve monthly payments. Recommend annual while clearly showing “£9.99 billed annually” and renewal/cancellation terms.

4. COST CHECK
100 monthly subscribers at 99p generate £99 gross/month. 100 annual subscribers generate £999 gross/year (£83.25/month spread over service year). Not profit/forecast.
Stripe UK standard-card processing published 1.5% + 20p, plus Billing pay-as-you-go 0.7% of Billing volume. Illustrative combined fees ~£0.22 per 99p charge and ~£0.42 per £9.99 charge (rounding/product/card caveats). Fixed fees consume ~22% monthly vs ~4% annual. Source https://stripe.com/gb/pricing
Before confirming viability include applicable tax, free users, retained cancelled-user photos, hosting/auth/database, photo storage and delivery, map/API requests, backups, email, support, refunds/disputes and any native-store fees. No surprise overage charges. Annual upfront cash still funds a year of service. Set usage alerts, finite allowances; avoid premature paid map layers/AI-heavy features at 99p.

5. STAGED DELIVERY
A. Review actual framework/hosting/auth/database/photo storage/export/map supplier. Identify existing vs incomplete features. Check fishery data licences, accuracy and freshness. Current seed dataset has 64 venues, 35 preview coordinates and 29 unknowns; approximate pins flagged. It is not a launch-ready exhaustive directory or entrance/navigation guide.
B. Prove core saving/ownership/recovery, interrupted-upload handling, export/restore and any local-to-cloud migration. Prior code review found silent localStorage quota failures; verify whether current build fixes them. Never assume success from old findings.
C. Design billing only after scope/cost decisions: hosted checkout and self-service billing portal for web/PWA. Review applicable store billing rules if native distribution planned. Have tax/consumer requirements checked appropriately.
D. After separate implementation approval, sandbox tests then limited paid pilot. Feature flag can stop new checkout without harming existing paid access or journal data.
Gates: no unresolved critical data-loss or cross-account defects; accurate useful Discover coverage in target area; owner approves prices, limits, provider, terms, retention and entitlements before build/launch.

6. SECURE BILLING/ENTITLEMENTS
Use server-owned entitlement per authenticated user: plan, provider customer/subscription IDs, status, paid-through date, cancel-at-period-end and last reconciliation. Client may read but cannot grant Premium.
Enforce Discover access on backend/API/data retrieval, not just hide tab. Check bundled public datasets, autocomplete, deep links and cached results for bypasses. Photo quotas server-enforced; journal ownership independent of billing.
Server creates checkout from approved price allowlist and authenticated account; don't trust client price/account IDs. Protect portal sessions against cross-account access; secrets never frontend.
Payment success redirect is not proof. Verify provider webhook signature on original body, persist event identifiers, handle duplicate/out-of-order events idempotently, reconcile authoritative subscription state and alert failures. Relevant Stripe events invoice.paid, invoice.payment_failed, customer.subscription.updated/deleted. Match invoice/customer/subscription and check state before entitlement extension. Define refund/dispute handling separately from subscription cancellation.
References: https://docs.stripe.com/billing/subscriptions/webhooks and https://docs.stripe.com/webhooks
Active paid: Premium through verified period. Scheduled cancellation: continue to period end. Incomplete initial payment: no Premium. Renewal failure: bounded grace only if approved. Expired/unpaid: Lite, no live Discover. Test refresh, direct links, multiple devices and stale cached entitlements. Already downloaded data cannot be assumed revocable.

7. PHOTOS/PRIVACY/DOWNGRADE
Private object storage with ownership checks and short-lived URLs. Validate file type/size; orient/compress photos and make thumbnails. Enforce byte quota atomically; concurrent uploads must not bypass. Handle abandoned uploads carefully.
Exact catches/swims private by default; remove unnecessary GPS metadata from shared variants. No precise locations/photos/journal text in payment metadata or ordinary analytics. Don't sell precise locations.
Downgrade removes live Discover but preserves journal/history/venue names. Existing photos remain accessible initially; block new photo uploads over Lite limit, allow reading/deleting/basic export. Practical export includes records and photos.
Approve sustainable cancelled/inactive retention before launch. Don't promise unlimited permanent storage or silently delete data; clear notice and export opportunity for future removal policy.

8. PILOT AND ACCEPTANCE TESTS
Measure first successful entry, repeat sessions, save/upload failures, Discover interest and use, checkout completion, paid conversion, renewal/churn/refunds/support and cost per account, without exact location analytics.
Directional pilot: 30 target UK coarse anglers recording two sessions and several independent paying customers after billing approval; record reasons to buy/decline. These are proposed validation goals, not guaranteed demand.
Required tests: Lite cannot call Premium endpoints; own journal always accessible; no cross-account reads; duplicate/out-of-order billing safe; upgrade/renew/cancel/failed pay/refund correct; downgrade preserves data; quota errors visible; interrupted uploads recover; restore/export demonstrated.
If users like diary but won't pay for Discover, improve directory value/coverage before adding complexity.
